Our Story

G2 Coffee Shop is a sample business created by Group 2 as an HTML and CSS website project. Our concept is centered on a welcoming neighborhood coffee shop where customers can enjoy coffee and spend quality time.

We believe that a coffee shop can be more than a place to buy a drink. It can be a comfortable space for people to relax, connect, study, and enjoy simple moments.
